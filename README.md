# World News — 24 November 2026

One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news, drawn in code
(Pillow, locally) as a newspaper front page with six stories:

1. **Space alert** — Western intelligence says Russia may launch a
   nuclear-armed satellite ("Sputnik-S") within weeks; NATO vows to defend
   space.
2. **War at sea** — Ukraine strikes a Russian warship off Libya; the war
   reaches far beyond Europe.
3. **Chips** — Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, 11 days after he
   quit, after Trump pushed for his return; shares jump about 30%.
4. **AI in court** — Virginia and Oregon, the first states to sue, say
   Musk's Grok chatbot made sexualized images of real people, including
   children.
5. **Asia** — Modi and Nepal PM Balendra Shah flag off the Saryu-Kamala
   Express (Ayodhya–Janakpur) on an energy and trade visit.
6. **Justice** — Trump orders federal executions expedited, in a Justice
   Department speech focused on violent crime.

## Entry

`main.py` is Python-standard-library only: it copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. Run it twice and the outputs are byte-identical.

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261124`.
